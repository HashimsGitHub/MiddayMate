### Find Someone New.

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Product Strategy Document

---

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term strategic direction of MiddayMate.

It answers the following questions:

* Why does MiddayMate exist?
* What problem are we solving?
* Who are our customers?
* Why will they choose MiddayMate?
* How will the platform generate revenue?
* How will the platform grow?

---

# Vision

To become the preferred platform for discovering people, places and promotions during the workday.

MiddayMate helps professionals make better use of coffee and lunch breaks while helping hospitality businesses attract new customers.

---

# Mission

Create meaningful real-world connections while driving measurable value to cafés and restaurants.

MiddayMate exists to encourage people to leave their desks, discover new venues and meet new people.

---

# Problem Statement

## Professionals

Many CBD professionals:

* Work long hours.
* Have limited opportunities to socialise.
* Eat lunch alone.
* Miss local venue promotions.
* Find traditional social and dating applications time-consuming and impersonal.

---

## Hospitality Businesses

Many cafés and restaurants:

* Experience quieter trading periods during weekdays.
* Need more effective local advertising.
* Want to attract nearby office workers.
* Need measurable returns on promotional campaigns.

---

MiddayMate brings these two groups together.

---

# Platform Philosophy

The venue is the starting point.

The social interaction follows naturally.

Rather than asking:

> "Who would you like to meet?"

MiddayMate asks:

> "Where would you like to go, and who would you like to invite?"

This creates value for both users and vendors.

---

# Guiding Principles

* Simple onboarding.
* Privacy-first.
* Mobile-first.
* Fast interactions.
* Real-world meetings.
* Vendor-funded platform.
* Minimal personal data collection.
* Build only features that create measurable value.

---

# Revenue Strategy

Users are free to use the platform.

Revenue is generated through hospitality vendors.

Primary revenue streams include:

* Featured venue listings.
* Sponsored campaigns.
* Promotional packages.
* Premium business profiles.

Future opportunities include:

* Campaign analytics.
* Loyalty programs.
* Reservation integrations.
* Event promotion.

---

# Competitive Position

MiddayMate is not intended to compete directly with traditional dating applications.

Instead, it combines elements of:

* Local venue discovery.
* Hospitality marketing.
* Social meetups.
* Professional lifestyles.

The platform differentiates itself by focusing on spontaneous real-world interactions during office hours.

---

# Privacy Strategy

Privacy is a core product value.

MiddayMate will:

* Collect only information necessary to operate the platform.
* Use OAuth authentication to simplify onboarding and discourage fake accounts.
* Avoid storing passwords.
* Avoid storing payment information.
* Store profile images separately from the application database.
* Give users control over optional profile information.

---

# Technology Strategy

The technology stack should remain intentionally simple during the MVP.

Current architecture:

* HTML5
* CSS3
* Vanilla JavaScript
* Python Flask
* SQLite
* Azure Blob Storage
* Docker
* Azure Container Apps

Future growth:

* Azure SQL Database
* Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)
* Native iOS and Android applications

Technology choices should always support product goals rather than drive them.

# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Phase 1

Launch within a single CBD.

Partner with a small number of cafés and restaurants.

Validate:

* User engagement.
* Venue participation.
* Campaign performance.

---

## Phase 2

Expand to additional CBD locations.

Increase vendor partnerships.

Introduce premium promotional packages.

---

## Phase 3

Expand nationally.

Introduce native mobile applications.

Increase venue categories and promotional offerings.

# Success Metrics

Success will be measured by outcomes rather than downloads.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

* Active users.
* Active vendors.
* Campaign impressions.
* Promotion redemptions.
* Invitations sent.
* Invitations accepted.
* Repeat venue visits.
* Daily active users.
* Monthly active users.
* Vendor renewal rate.

---

# Product Principles

Every new feature should answer at least one of the following questions:

* Does it help users discover people?
* Does it help users discover places?
* Does it create measurable value for vendors?
* Does it simplify the user experience?
* Does it encourage real-world interactions?

If the answer is **No**, the feature should not be included in the MVP.

# Long-Term Vision

MiddayMate aims to become the preferred platform for discovering hospitality experiences and spontaneous social opportunities during the workday.

The long-term objective is to build a sustainable marketplace where professionals benefit from meaningful connections and hospitality businesses benefit from increased customer engagement.
