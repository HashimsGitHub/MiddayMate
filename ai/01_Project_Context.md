# Project Context

## Product Overview
MiddayMate helps busy CBD professionals discover nearby cafés and restaurants, browse promotions, and arrange casual meetups during break times.

## Target Users
- Professionals seeking social or networking opportunities during the workday.
- Venue owners who want to attract more weekday traffic.
- Admins who need to moderate and manage the platform.

## Product Goals
- Make venue discovery fast and relevant.
- Encourage spontaneous in-person meetups.
- Help hospitality venues increase weekday engagement.

## Current Scope
The MVP should cover onboarding, profile creation, venue discovery, promotions, invitations, and basic chat.
